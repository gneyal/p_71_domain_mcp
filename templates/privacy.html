Privacy & Terms

Data Storage

We store your preferences, search history, and liked/disliked domains in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to our servers for storage.

How We Use AI

When you generate domain suggestions, we send your description and preferences to Anthropic's Claude AI to generate creative domain name ideas. This data is processed in real-time and is not stored by us.

Analytics

We use PostHog for basic analytics to understand how the service is used. This helps us improve the product. You can opt out of tracking by using browser privacy features.

Email Subscription

If you subscribe to daily recommendations, your email is stored in our database and used solely to send you domain suggestions. You can unsubscribe at any time.
